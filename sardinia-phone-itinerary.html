Punta Molentis is booking-only (2026)

Cap 150 people / 70 vehicles, €10, reserve by midnight the night before at pass.brav.it/Villasimius. Motorbikes count against the vehicle cap. Skipping it? Porto Giunco needs no ticket and is arguably prettier for a full day.

Logistics — parking, fuel, bookings
Parking

Beach lots fill early — park in sight of your towel, cover the hot seat, valuables on you. Confirm shaded bike parking at the hotel. At Olbia, follow the vehicle-boarding lane.

Fuel

Fri: fill before the Sarrabus. Sun: full tank leaving Villasimius and top up in San Teodoro, so you board with no errands.

Book (in order)
  • Saturday dinner
  • Marine-reserve boat (if Day 2 Option B)
  • Punta Molentis — only if you want it
  • Ferry QR ready on your phone
  • Check if Cala Brandinchi needs a day-ticket
Packing & heat
Bring
  • Reef-safe sunscreen, after-sun, wide-brim hat, polarised shades
  • Water shoes (rocky coves), quick-dry towel, dry bag
  • 2+ L water + electrolyte sachets on the bike
  • Packable umbrella for shadeless coves
  • Bungee cargo net for wet gear
  • Mesh jacket + gloves — abrasion doesn't care about heat
Heat
  • Ride 07:00–11:00; off the bike by midday. Never ride 12:00–16:00.
  • Hydrate before you're thirsty; add electrolytes
  • Soak a bandana / neck tube at every stop
  • Siesta guilt-free — the locals do
If weather or crowds change
  • Windy / rough sea: switch to sheltered coves — Cala Pira, Cala Caterina, the lee of Capo Carbonara.
  • Beaches packed / no parking: don't circle in the heat — pay for a sunbed, or go by boat.
  • Punta Molentis booked out: Porto Giunco — no ticket, arguably prettier.
  • Too hot midday: long shaded lunch + siesta, swim at 17:00 when the sand cools.
  • Sunday running late: cut the San Teodoro afternoon. The ferry is the one thing you can't miss (~2 h buffer built in).
